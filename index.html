FM-1 Switcher

Plug your FM-1 into your Mac over USB-C, pick a firmware, switch. Chrome or Edge only (Web MIDI).

Older FM-1 flash chip only

These firmwares only recognise the first FM-1s' Puya flash chip. On newer units (UC25HQ80, common on AliExpress) saving can fail and later updates may be refused. Use them only if you know your unit has the older chip.
