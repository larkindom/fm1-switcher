FM-1 Switcher

Older flash chip only, or unchecked

These firmwares only recognise the first FM-1s' Puya flash chip. On newer units (UC25HQ80, common on AliExpress) saving can fail and later updates may be refused. Firmwares marked unchecked aren't on the hub and haven't been vetted. Use these only if you know your unit has the older chip.

Backup & rescue tools

A web page can't read the whole flash. These tools can, and they cover the older-chip risk. Back up before you write anything to the FM-1.

  • M-Flasher: puts the FM-1 in download mode, saves the whole flash, installs a .fwsc. Works with broken firmware and the newer chip.
  • Melodee's fm1-recover: command-line recovery with a full backup first. Windows, Mac, Linux.
  • MvaveFM1Unbricker: full flash backup (read twice, compared), then write and verify.
  • The hub's Rescue: restores a hub firmware through the chip's bootloader (Linux, Python).
  • FM-1 Transporter: a small hardware board that reads and writes the whole flash.
More FM-1 tools
  • M-VAVE Workbench: editor for FM1 Open projects, sounds and samples.
  • FM1 Editor & Librarian: DX7 voice editor in the browser.
  • FM-1 Mirror: shows the FM-1's screen on your computer.
  • fm1buddy: a USB MIDI host box that also switches firmware from an SD card.
  • fm1-emulator: desktop emulator for Mac and Linux.
My saves

Backups of your project, samples and voice banks, kept in this browser and also downloaded as files. Restore works on the same firmware only. Drop an exported save into the box above to add it back.

Back up your projects and samples before switching: formats are not compatible between firmwares. Keep the FM-1 powered during a switch. Power loss mid-write can leave it unbootable.
