`jamesos-1.0a-build2105.fwsc` is `JamesOS-1.0a-build-2105-from-v15.fwsc` from https://gjelly.com/JamesOS (JamesOS 1.0a alpha, by Gjelly),
byte for byte (SHA-256 `fa6fb07ecfa0ae92a34ddd3c09b09b37308cf5f41384f4c14dd6535941a334e1`, the hash pinned in the author's installer; product `FM-1_9262105`).
GPL-3.0-only; built on Dinghy/Felucca/SLOOP, with Plaits/Braids (MIT). Pay-what-you-want on the author's site (the $0 option is offered); consider supporting it there.
Not on The Firmware Hub, so not vetted; flash-chip support is unknown (the author says newer-chip hardware still needs testing).
This is the "from V15" package: it writes a boot-area configuration for a 396 MHz profile, and the author's installer also adds a configuration marker
before writing. Other community firmwares may reject it; the author advises returning to official V15 first. Installing through this switcher is untested.
